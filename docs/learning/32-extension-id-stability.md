# 32. Extension-ID stability and identity continuity

## Established concept
Chrome extension IDs derive from identity material or unpacked-path semantics.
## Plain-language explanation
Identical files at different paths may become different extensions.
## Why AI agents struggle
Byte equality looks sufficient while browser identity has another input.
## Itzako example
Guard loads two exact copies and compares both IDs with `mofhg...`.
## Guard implementation
Static manifest-key evidence is combined with an isolated two-path experiment.
## Trade-offs
Empirical proof launches isolated browsers but does not touch normal Chrome.
## Failure modes
Assuming a canary ID or matching payload preserves normal-profile identity.
## Practical exercise
Predict and then measure IDs for two unpacked paths.
## Transfer
Test identifiers that depend on deployment path or environment.
## Key takeaway
Identity continuity must be proved across the actual replacement mechanism.
